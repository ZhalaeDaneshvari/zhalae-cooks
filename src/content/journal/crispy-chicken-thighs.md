I feel like I’ve really gotten this one down. Chicken thighs with paprika, cayenne, salt, pepper, hot sauce, and grated garlic. A lot of the things I reach for anyway, all in one dish.

## Get under the skin

I work the seasoning in with my hands, including underneath the skin. I want the chicken itself to have flavor, not just the outside. Then I let it marinate in the fridge.

I still eyeball the amounts, so this is my method rather than a fully measured recipe.

## Skin side down

I start the thighs skin side down in a pan over medium heat and let the fat render. I give them about 12 minutes in the pan, then finish cooking them in the oven. That pan time is approximate, not a test for whether the chicken is cooked through.

For doneness, [check for at least 165°F in the thickest part, away from the bone](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart). I still need to write down the oven temperature and timing before this becomes a full recipe.

## And then the potatoes

I love making these with roasted potatoes. The best part is taking some of the rendered chicken fat and spooning it over the potatoes for the last six minutes or so in the oven. It’s so good.

Use fat and juices from fully cooked chicken for that final spoonful, not raw marinade or drippings from chicken that’s still cooking. Six minutes alone isn’t a safety check.
