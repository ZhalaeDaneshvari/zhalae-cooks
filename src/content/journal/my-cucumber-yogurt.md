I call this my tzatziki, but it’s really my own version. I don’t use vinegar, and it feels a little closer to mast-o khiar, the Persian yogurt and cucumber dip. Whatever I call it, everyone seems to like it.

## Fresh dill is the best

Greek yogurt, Persian cucumbers, fresh dill, dried mint, olive oil, and salt. Those are the things I reach for here. I really love the fresh dill in it.

I dice the cucumbers into little pieces, but honestly, grating them is even better. That’s a specialty in my house. It mixes into the yogurt so nicely.

## Don’t forget the top

A little olive oil over the top, with salt and pepper, is one of my favorite parts. It sounds small, but it makes it so good.

And this is another dish where I keep tasting and adjusting the salt. I’ll mix it, taste it, add a little more, and suddenly all the flavor is there. Just go a little at a time so you don’t oversalt it.

## Chips, a drink, and you’re set

Plain potato chips or pita chips, some wine or whatever you like to drink, and a bowl of this. It’s such a good happy hour situation.

Between this and my garlic yogurt, there’s usually a dip someone is asking me to make again. I’m not complaining.

*About the name: [mast-o khiar is Persian yogurt and cucumber](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/food-network-kitchen/mast-o-khiar-18817089). This is my version, not a claim that there’s only one way to make it.*
