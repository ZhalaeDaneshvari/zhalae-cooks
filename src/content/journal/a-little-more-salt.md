Honestly, a lot of the time when my food tastes a little disappointing, it just needs more salt. Not another five spices. Salt.

I learned that from my dad, and then kept learning it for myself. You can put all these good things into a dish, but if it isn’t seasoned enough, the flavor just doesn’t quite show up.

## The tzatziki lesson

Every time I make tzatziki, I end up adding salt, tasting, and adding a little more. There’s a point where it all comes together and actually tastes like what I wanted. It really brings out the flavor.

The important part is the tasting. I’m not saying dump a bunch in and hope for the best. Add a little, mix it in, taste, and go from there. Enough salt makes a difference. Too much is still too much.

## Salt first. And don’t forget the pepper.

Steak is another one where salt is super important. My usual seasoning is coarse salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Nothing complicated, just enough to let the steak taste good.

I also really like pepper with turmeric. To me, it wakes up the flavor, and you’ll find that combination in my roasted potatoes. But even with all the paprika, garlic, and other seasonings I love, I still make sure there’s enough salt.

That’s the whole note, really. Before I decide something isn’t working, I taste it and ask whether it just needs a little more salt.
