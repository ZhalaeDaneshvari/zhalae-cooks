If you’ve eaten my food, you’ve probably had some combination of paprika, salt, pepper, cayenne, cumin, and turmeric. I reach for them all the time. Cumin especially shows up a lot.

I do a lot of Mediterranean dishes, and you can see those flavors across what I cook. I don’t really have a set formula for it. I eyeball things, taste, and figure it out as I go. Very much my dad’s way of cooking.

## A little of this, a little of that

That’s also why most things on here don’t have a written recipe yet. I know what I like, but I haven’t been keeping track of exactly how much of everything goes in. For now, these are the ingredients and habits I keep coming back to.

I’ll start writing some of it down. Until then, consider this a little peek into my kitchen, not a list of rules.
