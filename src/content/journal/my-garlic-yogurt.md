My friends and my dad ask for this all the time. It goes with steak, chicken, fish, chips, basically most things I want to put on the table. And it’s so simple.

## Good yogurt, lots of garlic

I start with good Greek yogurt, then add grated garlic, some onion powder, a little splash of olive oil, and salt. Mix it all together and that’s pretty much it.

I like it well salted. Not just a tiny sprinkle that disappears into the yogurt, but enough that you can actually taste everything. Add some, mix, taste, and keep going if it needs it. This is exactly what I mean in my salt post.

The grated garlic is the part I really love. Garlic powder has its place in my kitchen, but for this dip I want that fresh garlic flavor.

## Put it next to pretty much anything

A bowl of this next to steak or chicken makes me very happy. It’s also so good with chips. Sometimes the simplest thing on the table is the thing everyone keeps asking for.

I still eyeball it, so I don’t have exact amounts written down yet. Just good yogurt, garlic, a little olive oil, and enough seasoning to make it taste like something.
