Roasted potatoes are another one of my dad’s specialties. I’ve gone a little in my own direction with them, mostly by cutting mine thinner. Around a centimeter thick is what I aim for.

## Lots of garlic, always

I use grated garlic, turmeric, pepper, cayenne, paprika, and Italian seasoning. And, as always, enough salt. I’m eyeballing the seasoning, not measuring out a spice mix.

I love grated garlic here. It gives me that fresh garlic flavor I’m looking for, and pepper goes so well with the turmeric.

## Into the oven

My usual starting point is 375°F for about 45 minutes. I check that they’re tender and browned rather than treating the time as a guarantee. How thick I’ve cut them makes a difference.

If I’m making chicken thighs too, I spoon some fat from the fully cooked chicken over the potatoes for the last six minutes or so. Then they go back into the oven. That combination is one of my favorites.

These are still kitchen notes, not a fully measured recipe. But this is the version I keep coming back to, and I love it with the chicken thighs linked below.
