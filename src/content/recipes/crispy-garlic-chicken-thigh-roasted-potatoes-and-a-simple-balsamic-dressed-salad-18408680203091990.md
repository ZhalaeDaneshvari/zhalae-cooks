instructions:
  - "Pat the chicken thighs completely dry. Add them to a bowl with the olive oil, lime juice, hot sauce, paprika, cayenne, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, cumin, and grated garlic. Rub the marinade all over the chicken, making sure the skin is well coated. Cover and refrigerate for 35 minutes."
  - "While the chicken marinates, heat the oven to 375 F. Toss the potato slices with olive oil, Italian seasoning, thyme, paprika, turmeric, garlic powder, cayenne, lemon salt, salt, and black pepper. Spread them on a sheet pan in a single layer with no overlap."
  - "Roast the potatoes for about 45 minutes, turning or mixing them once halfway through so they brown evenly."
  - "When the potatoes are nearly done, heat a skillet over medium-low heat. Place the chicken thighs skin-side down and cook for 5 to 10 minutes, allowing the fat to render and the skin to become crisp. Flip the thighs and continue cooking until the total skillet time is about 12 to 15 minutes."
  - "Move the potatoes to one side of the sheet pan and place the chicken on the other side. Spoon the rendered chicken fat over the potatoes and lightly over the chicken. Return the pan to the oven for 6 to 8 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and reaches 165°F at the thickest part."
  - "While the chicken finishes, toss the arugula and cherry tomatoes with olive oil, balsamic glaze, salt, and black pepper. Add any other salad vegetables you like."
  - "Let the chicken rest for a few minutes, then serve with the roasted potatoes and dressed salad."
notes: Season the paprika and cayenne generously if you like more heat, but go lighter on the cumin. The chicken fat is what gives the potatoes their best flavor.
